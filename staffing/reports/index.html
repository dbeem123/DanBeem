Reports

Facility reports, with a source for every finding

Pick a Connecticut nursing home or management company and a report. Each finding is labeled as a confirmed fact, a calculation, a possible association, or an open question, and links to the public record it came from. You can send any report to your own AI assistant with one click.

Screening and research only. These reports join public CMS, DSS and DPH data. A shared name or principal is not proof of common ownership or a conflict of interest, and "not in these datasets" does not mean it did not happen.
