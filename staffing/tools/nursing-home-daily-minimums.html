How this works

Method and sources

  • Hours per resident day = nursing hours reported for the day ÃƒÂ· that day's resident census (CMS Payroll-Based Journal daily data).
  • All nursing counts every nursing position CMS counts: director of nursing, RNs and LPNs with administrative duties, RNs, LPNs, nurse aides, aides in training and medication aides. Direct care leaves out the director and administrative nurses.
  • Likely reporting problems: days under 1.0 nursing hour per resident are treated as incomplete payroll reports, not short staffing (this site's screening rule). A home with more than half its days like that is marked for the quarter.
  • Other anomalies: a day at least 25% below the home's own median for the quarter; census up or down more than 15% (and 5+ residents) in a day; more than 12 nursing hours per nursing worker; more than half of nursing hours from contract staff.
  • Weekend drop = how much lower weekend hours per resident are than weekday hours, for the quarter.
