Cost Report Insights

Trends and comparisons built from each home's own cost reports: how one home's spending changed over time and when owners or managers changed, homes side by side, management companies and chains as peer groups, a pivot table for any figure, spending against nursing care, and where each home's money goes. Every figure is per resident day, so large and small homes compare fairly. The year-by-year detail for each home is on Cost Reports.

These are the homes' own reported figures, before DSS audit. They are not a measure of care quality and are not rankings. A change that follows an ownership or management change is shown as timing only, not as its cause.
