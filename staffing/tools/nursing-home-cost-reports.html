Nursing Home Cost Reports

What each Connecticut nursing home reported to the Department of Social Services about its spending, staff, owners and related companies, cost years 2015 to 2025. Each year sits beside the home's CMS staffing for the same months, the management company and administrator of record, and CMS ownership and change-of-ownership records. Also see Management Companies, Ownership / Affiliation and the Administrator Search.

Figures are the home's own filing, before DSS audit or rate-setting adjustments. Spending is not a measure of care quality. Being listed as an owner or related party is not an allegation of wrongdoing.
