The Standard

Connecticut's direct-care staffing rules and when they applied

Connecticut now requires 3.00 hours of total nursing and nurse aide care per resident per day, of which 0.84 hours must be provided by licensed nursing personnel. The tools compare PBJ-derived estimates against these two points only for quarters when the standard applied.

  1. Before 2022
    Older regulatory baseline

    1.90 total and 0.64 licensed hours per patient day (Sec. 19-13-D8t(m)).

  2. 2021
    Statute directs a 3-hour standard

    Public Act 21-185 (CGS Sec. 19a-563h).

  3. March 1, 2023
    DPH 3.0 policies take effect

    0.84 nursing plus 2.16 nurse aide hours (3.00 total).

  4. January 5, 2024
    Amended DPH policies

    3.00 total, of which 0.84 licensed.

  5. January 27, 2026
    Staffing regulation approved

    3.00 total; licensed 0.57 by day and 0.27 overnight; an RN on duty at all times.

  6. February 11, 2026
    Staffing regulation takes effect

    Filed February 5; replaces DPH's staffing policies.
