DanBot SMS

DanBot is an AI assistant service operated by Daniel Beem for people Daniel authorizes who independently opt in. Users can send natural-language requests for conversational answers, authorized task assistance, and requested status updates. Image and song generation are planned/requested capabilities and are not represented here as verified SMS features.

How to opt in

  1. Send your message to +12135561964, DanBot’s SMS number.
  2. Open your SMS app using the button below. The message DANBOT will be prefilled for +12135561964.
  3. Confirm the destination is the number Dan provided, then press Send yourself.

Open SMS with DANBOT

Opening the composer does not send a message or provide consent. You opt in only when you press Send. The destination number is shown above so you can confirm it before pressing Send.

What to expect

Messages may include concise answers to natural-language questions, responses to authorized task requests, requested project or work-order status, and links to requested results when those capabilities are available. Message frequency varies with each person's interactions and requested assistant activity. Message and data rates may apply.

DanBot is not a marketing list and does not provide unsolicited messages. Texting DANBOT grants SMS consent only; it does not grant access to Daniel's private email, calendar, files, work information, or privileged tools.

Help and opting out

Reply HELP for help. Reply STOP at any time to opt out. After opting out, reply START or UNSTOP to opt back in through the carrier-supported flow.

Your choice

Consent applies only to the mobile number that sends the opt-in keyword. Consent is not a condition of a purchase and is not transferred to another person or number.
