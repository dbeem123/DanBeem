Nursing Home Management Companies

Organizations that Connecticut nursing homes reported to CMS as having operational or managerial control, the homes and dates involved since 2020, and each home's staffing and administrators during the management period. Also see the Administrator Search and Ownership / Affiliation.

Operational or managerial control is not the same as ownership or legal responsibility. Facility conditions during a management period reflect many factors and are not a measure of the management company's performance. Listing is not an allegation of wrongdoing.
