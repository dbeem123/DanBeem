Time and attention

Remember the next thing.

The BeemOps calendar surface is designed to make reminders, scheduled work, and project gates easier to see without creating a second scheduler or hiding what is actually authorized.

Back to BeemOps
Native authority: calendar views describe authorized reminders and scheduled workflows. External calendar changes, invitations, and other consequential actions remain separately gated.

  1. See what is next

    Upcoming reminders and project gates should be readable at a glance.

  2. Understand why it is there

    Each item should show its source, destination, schedule, and current state.

  3. Act carefully

    Run, pause, snooze, or change actions only when the authorized workflow supports them.
