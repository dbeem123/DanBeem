Little Things We Learned

Connecticut Company trolley No. 1160 was built in 1906. Many trolleys drew electricity from overhead wires. Woven rattan seats sometimes snagged clothing. Some trolley companies built amusement parks. Museums restore and preserve historic cars.

The Gregoire's Crossing dairy-farmer anecdote from the tour still needs documentary confirmation.
