CMS inspections, Connecticut

Inspections and Resident Rights

What state inspectors cited at Connecticut nursing homes, from CMS's public inspection files: health citations (F-tags), fire safety (K-tags) and emergency preparedness (E-tags). Explore citations about resident rights, see every citation on CMS's A to L scope and severity grid, follow each home's standard surveys and complaint investigations, and see how CMS scores a home's inspection record. Staffing, star ratings and fines for each home are on the Facility Explorer.

A citation is an inspector's finding that a home did not meet a federal requirement on the survey date. It is not a measure of care on any other day, and the number of citations depends on when and how often a home was inspected. These pages do not rank homes.

      How this is built

      • Citations come from CMS Health Deficiencies (F-tags) and CMS Fire Safety Deficiencies (K-tags and E-tags), with descriptions and categories from the CMS Citation Code Look-up. Survey dates and the risk-based survey footnote (29) come from CMS Inspection Dates. Scores come from CMS Provider Information.
      • Scope and severity letters: A to C mean no actual harm with potential for minimal harm; D to F, no actual harm with potential for more than minimal harm; G to I, actual harm that is not immediate jeopardy; J to L, immediate jeopardy. Within each level the letters go from isolated to pattern to widespread.
      • A tag is marked "cited before" when CMS's current file also shows the same tag at the same home on an earlier survey date. The file covers only about three years of standard surveys, so older repeats are not visible.
      • Resident rights topics group related tags. Each tag links to its guidance in Appendix PP of CMS's State Operations Manual. The grouping is the site's own and is listed in full on the Resident rights tab.
      • Correction dates are shown as CMS recorded them, including some that fall before the survey date.
      • Percentages and counts of homes say what they are out of: by default the Connecticut homes in the current CMS Provider Information file. The "Which homes are counted" tab explains the differences between CMS files.
