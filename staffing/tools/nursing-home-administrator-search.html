Nursing Home Administrator Search

Search a Connecticut nursing home administrator by name or license number to see each home where they were the administrator of record, with dates, and the home's staffing during that period. To see which organizations managed those homes, use Management Companies.

Facility conditions during an administrator's tenure reflect many factors and are not a measure of the administrator's performance. Listing is not an allegation of wrongdoing.
