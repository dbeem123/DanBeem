Read before using: PBJ staffing is quarterly screening data. CT comparison points are PBJ-derived screening estimates, not formal DPH compliance findings. Case-mix comparison points and CMS quality measures are contextual CMS values. Ownership and affiliation context is informational and does not prove common day-to-day control. Cost reports are each home's own filing to DSS, before audit; spending is not a measure of care quality. Use drill-down tools before drawing conclusions.
Historical CMS PBJ staffing is available from Q4 2017 through Q2 2026 in the facility history, change-over-time, and persistent-pattern views. Current CMS ratings, quality measures, affiliation context, and case-mix comparison points remain current snapshot context. DSS cost reports cover cost years 2015 to 2025 (years ending September 30).
