Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

This page describes the privacy practices for DanBot, an AI assistant service operated by Daniel Beem for people Daniel authorizes who independently opt in.

Information collected

The service may process an authorized user's mobile phone number, SMS message content, message timestamps, consent history, and technical delivery information as needed to send, receive, and operate personal assistant messages.

How information is used

Information is used only as needed to operate DanBot, including conversational answers, requested task assistance, requested status updates, and links to requested results when an applicable capability is available. Texting DANBOT does not grant access to Daniel's private email, calendar, files, work information, or privileged tools.

No sale or marketing sharing

Mobile phone numbers, SMS consent information, and message content are not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes.

SMS messaging

SMS messages are conversational and transactional only. Message frequency varies based on user initiated interactions. Message and data rates may apply. The user may reply STOP to opt out and HELP for assistance.

Service providers

Technical service providers, including Twilio for SMS transport and hosting or connectivity providers, may process information only as necessary to provide messaging, hosting, connectivity, or related infrastructure used by DanBot.

Capabilities and limitations

DanBot may support conversational answers, authorized task assistance, status updates, and requested creative results. Specific integrations, including image or song generation and result links, are staged capabilities and should not be treated as available unless the service confirms them.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be directed to the site owner through the GitHub profile associated with this site: https://github.com/dbeem123.
